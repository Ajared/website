What's Actually Working in AI

An opinionated read for enterprise teams. Models worth knowing, tools earning their keep, and what to skip.

01 The Models Worth Knowing
Claude
Best for: reasoning & writing
Long documents, nuanced instructions, careful reasoning. Strongest on safety-conscious or sensitive content.
GPT-4o
Best for: breadth & speed
Broad general tasks, coding, image understanding. Fast iteration. The most versatile all-rounder.
Gemini 1.5 Pro
Best for: long context
1M token context window. Ideal when your data lives in Google Workspace or you need to process very long docs.
Llama 3 / Open source
Best for: privacy & control
On-premise deployment. Use when you can't send data to a third-party API or need full control of the model.
02 Tools Earning Their Keep
Cursor / GitHub Copilot
Dev productivity
20–40% faster coding in real deployments. Fastest measurable ROI of any AI tool. Start here if you have a tech team.
Claude for Work / ChatGPT Teams
General assistant
Data privacy agreements in place. Solid for drafting, research, and summarisation across non-technical teams.
Notion AI
Knowledge mgmt
Useful for internal wiki search and doc summarisation. No IT required — easiest org-wide rollout.
Perplexity for Teams
Research
Replaces many Google rabbit holes. Sourced and real-time. Strong for sales teams and analysts.
Make / Zapier + AI steps
Automation
No-code AI automation that connects your existing tools. Start here before building anything custom.
03 What to Skip (For Now)
Building a custom LLM
Fine-tuning open-source models is almost always enough. Full custom training is for research labs with millions to burn.
Customer-facing chatbots first
High failure rate, high stakes. Start with internal use cases. Get good privately before going public.
Automating undocumented processes
AI accelerates documented processes. It doesn't rescue ones nobody understands. Write the SOP first.
Bulk tool purchases
One person using AI deeply beats 50 people with licenses they don't open. Adoption is the problem, not access.
Waiting for AI to mature
It is mature. What's available today is more capable than most organisations know how to use. Waiting is a competitive disadvantage.
