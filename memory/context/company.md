## Capabilities (Core Services)

### 001 — Applied Research
*"How We Learn What's True"*

Figures out what's actually going on before anyone starts building. Draws on behavioural science, statistics, and deep domain knowledge.

**Offerings:**
- UX Research — interviews, diary studies, usability tests, in-context observation
- Market Research — competitive analysis, market sizing, audience segmentation, trend mapping
- Testing Business Ideas — demand tests, concierge MVPs, assumption mapping, causal inference

**Process:** Scoping → Field Work → Analysis → Delivery

**Deliverables:** Research brief, structured findings report, exec presentation deck, evidence archive
**Optional:** Ongoing research retainer

---

### 002 — AI Product Development & Management
*"Strategy & Delivery"*

Full lifecycle AI product development from concept through launch and optimization. Everything under one roof.

**Offerings:**
- Concept Development — workshops, assumption pressure-testing, feasibility, strategic positioning
- Design Thinking — research-led UX, IA mapping, prototyping, user testing
- Product Management — roadmap, OKRs, prioritization, stakeholder alignment, GTM
- AI-Powered Engineering — full-stack dev, API integration, backend/frontend, QA, deployment

**Process:** Discovery → Design → Build → Launch

**Deliverables:** Concept brief, product strategy, roadmap, UX design system, working production product, launch plan, analytics instrumentation, 1 Month MVP Sprint
**Optional:** Fractional product management retainer

---

### 003 — Enterprise AI Agents
*"Autonomous AI Systems"*

Production AI agents that handle real tasks — not demos. Document processing, inter-system coordination, routine decision-making at scale.

**Offerings:**
- AI Agents — task-specific agents with tools, memory architecture, decision logic, human oversight
- Graph RAG — knowledge graph-augmented RAG for complex enterprise data, reduces hallucination
- Task Automation — end-to-end workflow automation across existing systems, handles volume + edge cases

**Process:** Discovery → Design → Build & Test → Deploy

**Deliverables:** Agent architecture doc, production-ready agent system with eval suite, integration layer + API docs, monitoring dashboard + alert config
**Optional:** Ongoing operations retainer

---

### 004 — Data & AI Strategy
*"Governance, Advisory and Adoption"*

Audits data, finds what's missing, builds a plan to get AI working inside an organization. Includes governance, adoption plans, and honest AI readiness assessments.

**Offerings:**
- Reliable AI — knowledge graphs and ontologies for consistent, trustworthy AI outputs
- Location Intelligence — geospatial data strategies, location-aware AI applications
- Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) — making the organization visible in AI-generated answers
- Executive AI Coaching — one-to-one advisory for leaders making AI decisions

**Process:** Audit → Assessment → Strategy → Handoff

**Deliverables:** Data audit report + gap analysis, AI readiness assessment + scoring, strategic roadmap + governance framework, exec presentation + adoption playbook
**Optional:** Ongoing advisory retainer
